## Why ECOTOX carries the soil argument

Residue testing measures what is on food. It does not measure effects on
non-target soil organisms — earthworms, collembola, microbial endpoints — at
all. ECOTOX is the federal source for those, which makes it the pillar that can
support a soil-and-ecosystem argument where PDP structurally cannot.

## The most persuasive toxicology is EPA's own

For a petition, the strongest material is usually EPA's own risk-assessment
language in the registration review dockets: human health risk assessments,
ecological risk assessments, and data evaluation records. That is where the
agency's own admissions live.

## Precedent to model on: chlorpyrifos

PANNA and NRDC petitioned in September 2007 under FFDCA §408(d) for revocation
of all chlorpyrifos tolerances plus FIFRA §6 cancellation. After years of
inaction the Ninth Circuit ordered EPA to respond by 2015-10-31; EPA proposed
revocation in November 2015 (80 FR, EPA-HQ-OPP-2015-0653), stating it could not
conclude aggregate exposure met the §408(b)(2) safety standard. Finalised 2021,
partially vacated by the Eighth Circuit 2023, further revocation action December
2024. Seventeen years, two circuits, one partial reversal. Plan the timeline and
litigation posture accordingly.
